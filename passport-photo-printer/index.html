Printing & Cutting Guide

  • Use Glossy Photo Paper (approx. 200gsm) for best results.
  • In Print Settings: Set Scale to 100% (Actual Size). Do not "Fit to Page".
  • Use a Steel Ruler and a Utility Knife for the cleanest cuts.
  • The gray "tick" marks guide your blade outside the photo area.
