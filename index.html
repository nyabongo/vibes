nyabongo/vibes

Small tools, vibe-coded.

A monorepo of standalone, single-file web tools. No build step, no dependencies — just open a folder.

Build or invest
Should a lump sum become an apartment block, or compound in the market? A crossover model for developers.
Brick by brick
Build a home a course at a time while you rent, or rent and invest instead? Works out when you move in and whether you ever finish.
Rent or buy
A crossover model comparing net worth over time for buying versus renting.
Source on GitHub. The calculators take their inputs from the URL — spec at llms.txt, so an AI assistant can hand you a filled-in link.
