nyabongo/vibes

Small tools, vibe-coded.

A monorepo of standalone, single-file web tools. No build step, no dependencies — just open a folder. Each calculator comes two ways: everything on one page, or a guided walkthrough that asks one question at a time and explains what each number means.

Build or invest
Should your money build a rental block, or just sit in the market? Models the construction drag, the lease-up, and what the finished block is worth.
Guided walkthrough →
Brick by brick
Should you build a home a bit at a time, or rent and invest instead? Works out when you move in and whether you ever finish.
Guided walkthrough →
Rent or buy
Should you buy a home, or rent and put the same money in the market? Shows the exact year buying overtakes renting — or that it never does.
Guided walkthrough →
Source on GitHub. The calculators take their inputs from the URL — spec at llms.txt, so an AI assistant can hand you a filled-in link. The guided walkthroughs read the same links.
